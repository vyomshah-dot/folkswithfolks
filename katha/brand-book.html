Katha

This document is an overlay. Katha inherits the full Folk with Folks Brand Book — palette, type, motifs, voice, logo geometry. The pages below capture only what is Katha-specific: the logotype, character badges, kit naming, packaging architecture, and product-line voice.

1. Architecture

Folk with Folks is the studio. Katha is the product line. Every Katha kit carries the endorsement line "a folk with folks kit" under the Katha logotype.

4. Kit naming

Pattern: <character>'s Kit of <trait>. Sentence case. Examples: Chanakya's Kit of Wit, Tenali's Kit of Mischief, Birbal's Kit of Cleverness.

5. Packaging

6. Voice flavour

Inherits the FwF voice (warm, curious, cheeky, sentence case, mostly "you", effectively no emoji). Leans a little more cheeky on the kit:
