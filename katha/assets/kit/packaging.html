One box, three lives

Katha 01 · packaging system

Sleeve · front

175 × 256 mm face · window 140 × 215

Rules

  • Indigo sleeve. The window is the hero: the real armature and clay are the picture.
  • Katha logotype in Fraunces italic, paper colour; "Wit" in marigold.
  • Endorsement "a folk with folks kit" in Hanken 600, tracked caps, top edge.
  • One torana arch, terracotta line, framing the window. No other motifs on the front.
  • Age 7+ in a ruled box, bottom right. Same corner on every Katha kit.

Finish

  • Matte, uncoated feel. Optional blind emboss on the torana line.
  • Window: die-cut; 155 × 230 mm clear acrylic or PET behind it, which becomes the display front.

Sleeve · back and sides

back 175 × 256 · sides 48.5 × 256

Back

  • Paper ground. Headline in Bricolage 800, two lines, the second in terracotta.
  • The 1 · 2 · 3 photo row tells the "three lives" story without words.
  • Legal block bottom-left. The ⚠ glyph is the only symbol allowed.

Spine (shelf face)

  • Terracotta for Chanakya, marigold for Tenali, neem for Birbal. A shelf of kits reads as a colour row.
  • Kit number at the bottom, character sun badge at the top.

Before print: check legal text with the printer (MRP, manufacturer address, toy-safety marking for India such as BIS / IS 9873 where it applies).

Inside prints

tray floor · tray inside · insert

Tray inside = work mat

Replaces the separate work mat. Grid, colour key, and a 1:1 skeleton outline so kids can check proportions.

Tray outside = stage floor

Plain paper deep with the 45 peg holes (4.2 mm) and a faint floorboard line. Nothing busy behind the puppet.

Insert

Unprinted 3 mm greyboard; piece letters laser-etched. Raw board reads as "workshop", and keeps cost down.

Backdrop

Indigo with one marigold stepped torana. Folds at 85 mm to store.
