Before you start

What's in the box

  • 1 wire skeleton
  • 30 cm spare wire
  • Clay: beige
  • Clay: brown
  • Clay: black
  • 3 stencils + base
  • 1 scraper
  • 1 foil sheet
  • 1 shawl (fabric)
  • 2 stage pegs
  • 8 shot cards
  • Backdrop
  • Phone stand (punch out)
  • Display window

⚠ Small parts. Not for children under 3. The wire ends have caps; keep them on. Clay is for building, not eating. Wash hands after.

Need a grown-up? Only for punching out the phone stand. Everything else is yours.

Chanakya outwitted kings. You're about to out-wit a cardboard box. Roughly the same energy.

Meet the skeleton

Bend the arms and legs gently. Never fold the same spot twice.

Why thin? Clay goes on top. Thin joints = a puppet that can still move.

Build3
2

Warm the clay

Roll each colour in your hands for one minute until it bends without cracking.

Too soft? Leave it in a cool place for 5 minutes. Too hard? Keep rolling.

Cold clay is grumpy. Warm clay listens.

Build4
3

Press, pop, wrap

Stencil 1: shapes ① legs and ③ arms. Press beige in, scrape flat, pop out, wrap.

Stuck? Bend the stencil a little and the piece slides out.

Build · Stencil 15
4

Torso

Use brown. Back piece on the spine, front piece on top. Press the edges together.

Leave the hips bare for now. The dhoti covers them on step 7.

Build · Stencil 16
5

Head

Scrunch foil into a ball. Wrap it with the two ④ halves. Press onto the head loop.

Made a wonky one? Good. Wonky has more personality.

Build · Stencil 27
6

Hands and feet

One piece under the loop, one on top. Squeeze. Keep the foot loop hole open.

Poke the foot hole through with a peg now. You will need it on step 9.

Build · Stencil 28
7

Make him Chanakya

Stencil 3. Black for shikha, brows, moustache. Beige dhoti. Drape the shawl last.

Dhoti pleats are already pressed in by the stencil. Don't smooth them away.

Build · Stencil 39
8

Set the stage

  1. Flip the box tray over. That's the stage.
  2. Slot the backdrop tabs into the two slits.
  3. Punch out the phone stand. A + B + 3 bars. No glue.
  4. Push the 2 pegs up through the feet holes.
Shoot10
9

Shoot the story

  1. Phone in the stand. Pick a notch: 80° faces him, 50° looks down.
  2. Lock focus and light: tap and hold on Chanakya.
  3. Move one thing a tiny bit. Take 2 photos. Repeat.
  4. 12 photos = 1 second. A shot card = about 24 photos.

Never touch the phone between photos. Use a timer or headphone button. Any stop-motion app works.

Shoot11
10

Keep him

Stand the sleeve on its end. Slide in the clear window. Chanakya lives here now.

Young Chandragupta lost every battle by attacking the capital first. A mother told her son: don't grab the hot centre of the khichdi, eat from the cooler edges. Chanakya heard. The empire fell edge first.
