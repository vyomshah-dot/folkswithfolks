# Katha 01 (Chanakya) — kit cut files

First-pass, measured drawings for one prototype. Every file is an SVG at 1 unit = 1 mm.
They are generated by `tools/gen-kit-cutfiles.py`; change a number there and re-run it
rather than editing the SVGs by hand.

**Nothing here has been cut or tested yet.** Treat every dimension as a starting point
for the first prototype.

## Line colours

| Colour | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Red | Cut through |
| Green (dashed) | Fold / score |
| Blue | Raised ridge or line on the stencil base plate (not cut in the frame) |
| Black | Etched or printed label / guide, never cut |

## How the stencils work

1. The frame (the red shapes) sits on a flat base plate.
2. Press plasticine into a shape, scrape it flat, pop the piece out. Every piece is 3 mm thick.
3. Blue strips are ridges on the base plate, 2.5 mm wide and about 1 mm high. They leave a
   groove in the piece where the armature wire sits, so the piece folds shut around the limb.
4. Numbers etched beside each shape match the manual steps.

Sizes assume the 19 cm × 6 cm twisted-wire armature and a 3 mm clay sheet:
finished arm about 10 → 8 mm thick, leg 13 → 10 mm, head 30 mm around a 24 mm foil ball.

## How the phone stand works

- Two side ribs (A and B), a top bar, and front and rear spreaders. No glue.
- The phone's bottom edge sits in one of four notches (13 mm wide, fits phones with a case)
  and its back leans on the top bar.
- The notch sets the lean: about 80°, 70°, 60° or 50° from the table. These are calculated
  for a thin phone; a thick case leans a few degrees less.
- Ribs are 47 mm apart, so the phone can sit upright or sideways.
- Slots are drawn 3.0 mm wide for 3 mm board. Adjust `T` in the script for the real board
  and the laser's kerf.

## To check on the first prototype

- Foot-loop opening on the armature against the 5.2 mm peg holes.
- Whether the two head halves close cleanly around the foil ball.
- Stencil pop-out: plasticine may need a light dusting of talc or a flexible frame.
- Slot tightness on the phone stand, and whether the 80° notch is stable with a tall phone.
- Box sizes and closures with a packaging vendor; corner tabs can be tucked or glued.
