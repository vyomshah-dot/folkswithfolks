The Idea

One house, many tales.

Folk with Folks is the studio. It exists to put Indian folk stories back into people's hands — literally — through puppets, kits, films and workshops. The name carries its whole job: folk (the tradition, the craft, the characters) brought to folks (families, kids, the curious), and made with folks, not for them.

The mark means

A stepped torana — the temple/festival arch, doubling as a puppet stage — framing two Warli figures joined at the hands. Folk craft, the stage in every Katha kit, and people together, in one mark.

The feeling

Handmade, warm, a little wonky. Block-print and clay, not glossy plastic. Credible enough for a studio, playful enough for a seven-year-old.

The promise

Tales you can hold. Every product turns a story into something a child builds with their own hands.

Do
  • Keep clear space of one figure-height on all sides.
  • Minimum size: symbol 24 px / 9 mm; full lockup 120 px / 30 mm.
  • Place on paper, ink, terracotta, or a calm photo area.
  • Use mono version for emboss, single-colour print, stamps.
Don't
  • Recolour the figures outside the palette or add gradients.
  • Stretch, rotate, or add drop-shadows / outer glows.
  • Place on busy patterns or low-contrast backgrounds.
  • Re-typeset the wordmark in a different font.

Type

Three voices.

A modern grotesque with character does the talking; a warm literary serif tells the tales; a clean humanist sans keeps instructions legible for small hands and tired eyes. All three are free on Google Fonts.

Display — Bricolage Grotesque · 600 / 800
Tales you can hold
Headlines, the wordmark, product names, posters. Set tight (letter-spacing −2 to −3%). ABCDEFG abcdefg 0123456789
Story / Quotes — Fraunces · italic
"Once, in the court of a clever king…"
Story openings, pull-quotes, the "with" in the wordmark, captions with warmth. Italic for flourish, roman for longer reads.
Body / UI — Hanken Grotesk · 400 / 600

Twist the wire into a loop for the head, then a longer length for the spine. Press the foil around it, a little at a time, until your puppet has shoulders. This is the workhorse: manuals, instructions, captions, web, decks — anything someone has to actually read and follow.

Scale (web px): H1 64 · H2 44 · H3 24 · lead 21 · body 17 · caption 13. Line-height ~1.6 for body, ~1.05 for display.

Voice

How it talks.

We are

Warm, curious, a touch cheeky. We treat kids as clever and adults as kids-who-forgot. We tell, we don't lecture. Short sentences. A wink, never a sneer.

We aren't

Preachy, precious, or "educational" in the dull sense. No corporate craft-speak. No earnest heritage worship — folk culture is alive and funny, not a museum.

"Chanakya outwitted kings. You're about to out-wit a cardboard box. Roughly the same energy."
